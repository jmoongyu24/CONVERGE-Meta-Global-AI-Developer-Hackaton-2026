# Weekly 03 — proposals

2026-09-09, Wednesday 14:00–15:40 · all four of us · notes by Mina

Leo presented the role-negotiation proposal first. Before solving, agents run two rounds of role bidding: each agent states which sub-task it wants and why, then a short vote assigns roles. No training, any base model.

Jae had already run a quick pilot for Leo on two tasks, and the negotiated team beat the fixed-role baseline on both (+6% success on average). Jae thinks this is promising enough to extend to all five tasks.

I raised the obvious concern: prompted negotiation looks a lot like the role-play frameworks from 2023 (CAMEL etc.), and reviewers will ask what is new. I also doubt it scales — with more than five agents the bidding rounds will turn into noise.

Prof. Han asked the same novelty question more neutrally: what exactly is the contribution beyond prompting? Asked Leo for a related-work table before we invest more.

Then my proposal: same RL idea as last week but with the 6-template action space, which cuts the estimate to about 4 GPU-days per task family. Leo still thinks the reward signal will be too noisy. Prof. Han said the reduced cost makes it worth a small pilot.
