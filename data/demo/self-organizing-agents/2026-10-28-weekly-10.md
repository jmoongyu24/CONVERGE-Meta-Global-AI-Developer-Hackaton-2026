## Results (Jae)
- Mid-episode shift: negotiation recovers within 2 rounds; the bandit needs a new episode to re-select; the hybrid recovers in 3 rounds.
- Scale: with more than 6 agents, negotiation becomes unstable — 40% of runs end without an agreed role assignment.
- Cost: bandit is cheapest; hybrid +9% tokens; negotiation +18%.

## Discussion
- Jae: My proposal to fix the scale issue: hierarchical negotiation. Sub-teams of at most 4 negotiate internally, then sub-team leads negotiate with each other.
- Leo: Fully support that. It keeps negotiation training-free and solves the 6-agent problem.
- Mina: If negotiation breaks at 6 agents, it is not self-organization at scale. The bandit has no such limit.
- Leo: And the bandit cannot react mid-episode, which is the evaluation we all agreed matters most.
- Prof. Han: Through the budget lens: bandit is the cheapest, the hybrid the most robust, negotiation the strongest on shifts. Each of you can claim a win; that is the problem.
