Yuki presented the combined method (calibrated randomization) on hardware: brass 63%, printed pegs 58%, aluminium 69%. It is never the best on a single object but never bad either. Yuki thinks the residual model is still the cleaner story for tight tolerances and would build the paper around it.

We all agreed on one thing: the paper must include the held-out object results, whatever the main claim is.
